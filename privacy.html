Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-19

GTA 6 Clips runs locally on the developer's Windows PC. It does not operate a public server, sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Information stored locally

The app stores settings, clip metadata, local file paths, and any TikTok authorization token in its local data folder on that PC. These files are not published by these web pages.

Information sent to TikTok

When you click the TikTok upload button, the authorization token and the selected video are sent to TikTok's official API endpoints so the video can be posted to the authorized account. TikTok's privacy policy applies to that processing.

Your control

You can revoke the app's TikTok access at any time from TikTok's authorized apps or security settings. Deleting the local data folder removes the locally stored settings and token.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the email address associated with the TikTok developer application.
